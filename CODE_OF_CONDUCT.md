# Code of Conduct

Telltale adopts the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. The linked text is the normative document; the summary below is only a quick reference.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and assume good intent, especially with people asking their first question.
- Give and accept technical feedback about the code, not about the person writing it.
- Keep discussions on the project: bugs, sensors, the panel, the docs.
- Respect different skill levels and different hardware; "it works on my machine" is data, not an argument.
- Credit other people's work when you build on it.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks in issues, pull requests or discussions.
- Discriminatory comments about who someone is or where they come from.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly derailing threads or drowning out other voices.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.

## Enforcement

Report incidents privately to the maintainer, [@barraco002](https://github.com/barraco002), through GitHub: a private message, or a private security advisory on https://github.com/barraco002/telltale. Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove contributions and may block accounts that keep violating this code.
